Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What this covers

This policy applies to a personal Google OAuth client that I, Insee Thaopech, use with rclone to back up and sync my own Google Drive files. It is not offered to the public and is used only by me.

Data accessed

The client accesses Google Drive files in my own account, with the permissions I grant when signing in. File data moves only between Google's servers and machines I control.

Data collection and sharing

I do not collect, store, sell or share any data with third parties. No analytics or tracking is attached to the client. OAuth tokens are stored locally in my rclone configuration.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
